# SLS 81: Ceylon Cinnamon Specification and Quality Standards

## 1. Overview
Ceylon Cinnamon (*Cinnamomum zeylanicum Blume*), also known as True Cinnamon, is native to Sri Lanka. SLS 81 is the national quality standard published by the Sri Lanka Standards Institution (SLSI). Ceylon Cinnamon is prized globally for its low coumarin content (< 0.004%), sweet aroma, and thin, soft, light-brown layered quills.

## 2. Grade Classification
Ceylon Cinnamon quills are classified into four main categories:
1. **Alba**: The highest grade of cinnamon. Diameter up to 6 mm. Soft, uniform quills made of very thin inner bark.
2. **Continental**:
   - C5 Special (diameter 6 - 10 mm)
   - C5 (diameter 10 - 12 mm)
   - C4 (diameter 13 - 15 mm)
3. **Mexican**:
   - M5 (diameter 16 - 19 mm)
   - M4 (diameter 19 - 22 mm)
4. **Hamburg**:
   - H1 (diameter 23 - 25 mm)
   - H2 (diameter 25 - 32 mm)

## 3. Physical & Chemical Requirements
- **Moisture Content**: Maximum 15.0% by mass.
- **Volatile Oil**: Minimum 1.0% (v/w) for Alba and C5 Special; minimum 0.7% for lower grades.
- **Extraneous Matter**: Maximum 1.0% by mass.
- **Coumarin Level**: Must not exceed 40 mg/kg (0.004%), distinguishing Ceylon Cinnamon from Cassia (*Cinnamomum cassia*) which contains up to 1% coumarin (toxic to liver in high doses).

## 4. Packaging & Export Marking
- Quills must be packed in clean, dry jute bags or food-grade polyethylene liners.
- Marking must include: SLS Mark, Grade designation, Lot number, Net Weight, Country of Origin ("Pure Ceylon Cinnamon"), and Exporter EDB Registration Number.
